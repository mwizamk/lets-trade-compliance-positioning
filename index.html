01 / BUSINESS MODEL

The safest positioning

The business can generally be structured around helping customers understand, choose, purchase and set up legitimate subscriptions. The critical distinction is between providing independent assistance and falsely claiming to be the underlying provider.

Recommended positioning

Let's Trade ZM is an independent digital subscription assistance service.

A

Assisted purchase

Customer → Let's Trade ZM → appropriate official provider → customer's subscription.

Lowest-risk model

B

Authorized reseller/partner

Provider → authorized agreement → Let's Trade ZM → customer.

Best when formally authorized

C

Reselling purchased subscriptions

Whether this is permitted depends on the provider's terms, reseller rights, account rules and applicable law.

Verify first

D

Shared accounts / profiles

Do not build the business around this unless the provider explicitly permits the arrangement.

High-risk model

06 / CUSTOMER TRUST

Independent Service Notice

Independent Service Notice

Let's Trade ZM is an independent digital subscription assistance service and is not Netflix, Spotify, Amazon, Showmax, Apple, Google, or any other third-party service provider unless explicitly stated. Brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. We help customers understand available services and, where permitted, assist with subscription purchasing and setup.

Operating rules

  • Never claim to be an official provider unless authorized.
  • Never imply a partnership, certification or reseller status that does not exist.
  • Clearly tell customers when they are paying Let's Trade ZM rather than the underlying provider.
  • Describe exactly what the customer receives.
  • Keep prices, refund terms and service descriptions accurate.
  • Do not build the business around unauthorized account sharing.
  • Respect platform advertising, trademark, messaging and community rules.
  • Verify provider terms before reselling, transferring or sharing subscription access.
  • Obtain local professional advice before scaling a business model that depends on resale or handling customer payments.
